Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Your input

The tools run in your browser. Text you type and images you choose are processed on your device and are not sent to Toolora servers. The site has no accounts, no database and no file uploads.

Hosting logs

The site is served by a static hosting provider. Like most web hosts, it may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. Its own privacy policy applies to that data.

Advertising

Toolora may display advertisements from Adsterra. Ad networks and their partners can use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads, and may collect data such as IP address and device information. See Adsterra’s privacy policy for details. You can limit this through your browser settings or ad-choice tools.

Storage on your device

Toolora saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage. Nothing else is stored.

External resources

Most pages load no third-party files. The QR Code Generator and the PDF tool pages load open-source libraries (qrcode-generator, pdf-lib, PDF.js) from the jsDelivr CDN, which may log the request. Advertising code, when added, loads from the ad network.
