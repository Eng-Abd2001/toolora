Questions

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The tools run in your browser and your input stays on your device. Our privacy policy covers hosting logs and advertising.

Does Toolora cost anything?

No. The site is free to use and is supported by advertising.

Are there image or PDF tools?

Yes. Image tools and PDF tools are included. The PDF tools load open-source libraries from a CDN, but your files are not uploaded.
