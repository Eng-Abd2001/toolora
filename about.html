About Toolora

Toolora is a collection of small utilities for jobs that come up again and again: counting words, cleaning a list, generating a password, checking a JSON file. Each tool does one thing, runs in your browser and says plainly what it cannot do.

The site is free and is supported by advertising. If something is wrong or missing, tell us.
